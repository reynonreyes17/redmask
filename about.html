About Milky Makers

The Name

Milky speaks to richness, nourishment, and the kind of deep softness that healthy skin is known for. Makers is about intention — crafted deliberately, not mass-produced. Because everything we do at Milky Makers is about what your skin can become when it's genuinely fed, not just treated on the surface.

Milky Makers was built in the UAE, for the UAE. Not adapted from a brand designed for a different climate and a different consumer — built here, for the specific realities of living and caring for your skin in this part of the world.

Why We Exist

The UAE is an extraordinary place to live. It is also an extraordinary environment for your skin to navigate. The UV Index regularly exceeds 10 — classified as extreme. Air conditioning runs continuously, stripping moisture from your skin for 18 hours a day. Humidity swings between 90% in August and 25% in February. Hard water disrupts your skin's pH. And professional skincare treatments cost hundreds of dirhams per session — making expert-level care inaccessible for most people on any kind of budget.

Milky Makers was founded because there was no skincare brand on the UAE market that addressed all of this honestly, affordably, and with the ingredient quality to justify the claims being made.

Our formulas are built around milk-derived actives — ingredients clinically recognised for their ability to deeply nourish, repair the skin barrier, and restore the kind of softness that the UAE climate works hard to take away. Rich in lactic acid, peptides, and proteins, milk has been used for skin health for centuries. We took that heritage and built a modern, science-backed skincare line around it.

Our Ingredients

We don't use milk as a marketing story. We use it because it works.

🥛

Lactic Acid

Naturally occurring in milk, it is one of the gentlest and most effective exfoliants available. It dissolves dead skin cells, brightens uneven tone, and stimulates collagen production — without the irritation that harsher acids cause.

🧬

Milk Proteins

Strengthen the skin barrier — the invisible shield that keeps moisture in and irritants out. When that barrier is compromised, skin becomes reactive, dull, and dry. Our formulas are designed to rebuild it, session by session.

💧

Peptides

Signal the skin to produce more collagen and elastin — the proteins responsible for firmness and bounce. In the UAE climate, where collagen is depleted faster by UV stress, this is not optional. It is essential.

✨

Ceramides

Lipid molecules that lock moisture into the skin and form a protective layer against environmental damage. Combined with milk proteins, they create a barrier strong enough to hold up against air conditioning and desert air.

Clinically Considered

Every Milky Makers product is formulated with reference to peer-reviewed research on milk-derived actives and skin barrier science. We don't make claims we can't back up. We don't add ingredients for marketing value. Everything in the formula is there because it has a demonstrated role to play in healthier, more resilient skin.

Lactic acid's efficacy as an AHA exfoliant is one of the most extensively documented in dermatology. Milk protein's role in barrier repair is supported by decades of cosmetic chemistry research. When we say our formulas are science-backed, we mean it in the literal sense — not as a brand promise dressed up as evidence.
